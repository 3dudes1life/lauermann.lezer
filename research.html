RESEARCH QUEUE

Questions worth pursuing.

Each open box states what is unknown, why it matters, and which record would change the evidence status.

01 · LAUERMANN · HIGHEST PRIORITY

Find Peter Lauermann II’s 1794 baptism

Search the Wadern/Büschfeld Catholic parish context for the reported 16 June 1794 birth/baptism. Parents and their marriage are the gateway to earlier generations.

02 · LEZER / GRABINSKI

Prove Fred Lezer’s birthplace and parents

Use U.S. bridge records, immigration/naturalization sources, then East Prussian/Polish civil and parish evidence to test the Kurkau/Neidenburg lead.

03 · SPOO / GROSSMANN / FREYWALD

Establish Richard Spoo’s parish and locality

Resolve Richardus/Richard naming, identify the original register, and test the Grossmann/Freywald parentage chain with records rather than compiled trees.

04 · LIMPERICH / HERSCHBACH / ASSENMACHER

Identify exact German villages

Locate original baptism, marriage, and burial entries before assigning any village, parish, or German origin to the bridge generations.

05 · BRENNAN / WALSH / WARREN

Test the Warren extension

Prove or disprove the proposed James Warren (1682–?) line. The existing claim remains a tree-only lead until original evidence connects it.

06 · VETERANS & SERVICE

Search all families for service records

Run a dedicated search across Lauermann, Lezer, Braman, Brennan, Walsh, Warren, Grossmann, Spoo, Freywald, Frings, Limperich, Herschbach, Assenmacher, and Grabinski.

EVIDENCE STANDARD

Four ways the archive speaks.

Supported now

PROBABLE

Strongly supported

Multiple facts fit, but the decisive original record is missing.

RESEARCH LEAD

Worth testing

FAMILY MEMORY / CONFLICTING

Preserved, not flattened
