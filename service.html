SERVICE & LEGACY

Navy service, then a life on the railroad.

JANUARY 1942

Enlists in the U.S. Navy

Edward entered service in January 1942 and trained at Great Lakes.

WORLD WAR II SERVICE

Service aboard USS Gantner (DE-60) and USS Hugh Purvis (DD-709)

The archive records these vessels as documented service assignments. It does not infer combat, campaign participation, medals, or duty details beyond the evidence currently held.

26 JANUARY 1946

Honorably discharged

Discharged as Fireman First Class.

29 JANUARY 1946

Railroad career begins

Great Northern employment began three days after discharge, employee no. 834746. Payroll and retirement records, later Burlington Northern material, and BLE Division 143 items trace a career of roughly four decades.
