VETERAN PROFILE · DOCUMENTED

Edward J. “Ed” Lauermann

1925–2012 · married Anamae Lezer in 1950

BRANCHUnited States Navy

TRAININGGreat Lakes
DISCHARGE26 January 1946 · Fireman First Class · honorable

SERVICE & LEGACY

Navy service, then a life on the railroad.

JANUARY 1942

Enlists in the U.S. Navy

Edward entered service in January 1942 and trained at Great Lakes.

WORLD WAR II SERVICE

Service aboard USS Gantner (DE-60) and USS Hugh Purvis (DD-709)

The archive records these vessels as documented service assignments. It does not infer combat, campaign participation, medals, or duty details beyond the evidence currently held.

26 JANUARY 1946

Honorably discharged

Discharged as Fireman First Class.

29 JANUARY 1946

Railroad career begins

The strongest working source gives 26 January 1946 as Edward’s Great Northern start date; an earlier working note gives 29 January. Railroad personnel and retirement records are needed to settle that difference. Employee no. 834746, later Burlington Northern material, and BLE Division 143 items trace a career of roughly four decades. He retired on 31 March 1986 and later drove a school bus for about 19 years.

TWO KINSHIP STORIES, SHOWN SIDE BY SIDE

ADOPTIVE FAMILY

Lauermann / Brennan

Edward was adopted in 1929 by Charles Joseph Lauermann and Margaret Cecile Brennan. These are recorded as adoptive relationships, not silently merged with biological ancestry.

REPORTED BIOLOGICAL FAMILY

Braman / Brennan

He was reportedly born to George Braman and Clara Brennan. These connections are displayed as reported biological relationships pending the archive’s supporting documents.
