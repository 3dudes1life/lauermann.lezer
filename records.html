MEDIA RULE

What an image can—and cannot—prove.

The supplied 24-image pack appears throughout this archive as clearly captioned illustrative period imagery. It supports a historical reading experience but is never used as a family photograph, original record, or proof of a claim.

No authentic family portrait or record scan was included in the supplied asset pack. When one is provided, it will appear with an “Original family photo” or “Original record” label, source/provenance, and a description distinct from the illustration gallery.
