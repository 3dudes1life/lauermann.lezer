GENERATIONS IN CONTEXT

A working family timeline.

Dates track the archive’s current record, not a claim of a complete or proven lineage. Earlier points marked as leads stay visibly tentative.

c. 1702 · RESEARCH LEAD

Nicolaus / Nicolas Peter Lauermann

Compiled-tree lead clustered around the Büschfeld/Wadern area. The relationship to Peter II is not proven.

1720–1682 · TREE-ONLY LEADS

Walsh / Warren extension

William Walsh, Maria Warren, and James Warren appear in compiled material; the 1682 James Warren line is unverified.

c. 1756 · RESEARCH LEAD

Richardus / Richard Spoo

Needs parish and locality evidence. “Richardi” may be Latin grammar rather than a middle name.

1780–1789 · PROBABLE

Patrick Brennan, Catherine Walsh, Mathias Grossmann, Gerdrudis Freywald

Working-generation entries awaiting original parish linkage.

16 JUNE 1794 · DOCUMENTED ANCHOR / BAPTISM SOUGHT

Peter Lauermann II, reported birth

The Wadern-area Catholic baptism remains the archive’s highest-value missing record.

1805–1811

John Brennan; Nicolai Spoo; Heinrich Herschbach; Susanna Assenmacher

Bridge generations shown in the current research model; exact localities and original records remain needed for several lines.

1823–1866

Peter Lauermann, Peter J. Limperich, Bartolomaeus Grossmann, Catharinae Spoo, Margaretha Limperich

European-to-American bridge generations across the German family chapters.

1881–1895

Fred Lezer lead; Charles Joseph Lauermann; Margaret Cecile Brennan

Fred’s reported Kurkau/Neidenburg origin needs proof. Charles and Margaret later became Edward’s adoptive parents.

1925–1950

Edward J. Lauermann and Anamae Lezer

Edward’s Navy service began in 1942; postwar railroad employment began 29 January 1946; Edward and Anamae married in 1950.
