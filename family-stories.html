EDWARD J. “ED” LAUERMANN

Born a Braman, raised a Lauermann.

Edward was born on 3 January 1925 in St. Cloud to George A. Braman and Clara Brennan Braman. In 1929, Charles J. Lauermann and Margaret Cecile Brennan Lauermann adopted him. Research suggests Clara and Margaret were sisters, which would mean Edward was raised by his maternal aunt and uncle; that relationship still needs the adoption and vital records that can confirm it.

FROM NAVY BLUE TO RAILROAD STEEL

Edward’s working life.

1942–1946

U.S. Navy

Edward trained at Great Lakes and served aboard USS Gantner (DE-60) and USS Hugh Purvis (DD-709). He was honorably discharged on 26 January 1946 as Fireman First Class.

POSTWAR YEARS

Great Northern and Burlington Northern

Edward’s railroad papers identify employee no. 834746 and a career of about four decades. The working files disagree on whether he began 26 or 29 January 1946; the personnel record is the next document to find.

31 MARCH 1986

Retirement

Edward retired from the railroad in 1986 and later drove a school bus for about 19 years.

25 FEBRUARY 2012

Remembered in St. Cloud and Sauk Rapids

He died at the St. Cloud VA Medical Center, according to the current obituary-based research, and is buried at Trinity Lutheran Cemetery in Sauk Rapids.

ANAMAE LEZER LAUERMANN

Her own story in Sauk Rapids.

Anamae, born 18 October 1928, was not only Edward’s wife. She worked at the Dairy Bar, the Social Security office, and in manufacturing, and was active at Trinity Lutheran Church. She and Edward married on 28 October 1950 at St. Augustine’s Parish House in St. Cloud.

THE LEZER HOUSEHOLD

The sisters of Sauk Rapids.
